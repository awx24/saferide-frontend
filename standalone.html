Platform Features

Everything Your Family Needs

One platform for parents, drivers, and schools — designed around Pakistani school routes, languages, and payment systems.

📍

Real-Time GPS Tracking

Watch the school van move live on an interactive map. Automatic ETA updates, route deviation alerts, and stop-by-stop confirmations.

🚐

Driver Registration & Verification

Multi-step CNIC, license, and vehicle document submission. Police clearance integration. Full background check before first ride.

👨‍👩‍👧

Parental Control Panel

Track vans, set custom geofence safe zones, manage child profiles, restrict routes, and get push notifications for every milestone.

📋

Live Attendance — حاضری

Driver taps to mark pickup and drop-off. Parents and schools get instant WhatsApp + in-app notifications. Daily PDF reports available.

🆘

SOS Emergency Alert

One tap sends GPS location, van photo, and alert to parents, school admin, and local police. Connected to Rescue 1122 & Edhi.

💳

JazzCash / Easypaisa Pay

Monthly van fees paid digitally. No cash. Automatic WhatsApp receipts. Split payments, advance booking, and instalment support.

📊

Analytics Dashboard

School admins see daily attendance trends, route efficiency, driver ratings, and revenue reports — all in visual, shareable dashboards.

🌐

Urdu Interface

Full Urdu language support for drivers and parents who prefer communicating in their native language. RTL layout included.

🛡️

Insurance & Safety Guarantee

Every trip covered by basic passenger insurance. Vetted drivers only. School confirmation required before van allocation.
